# Lull privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

Lull does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

- **No internet access.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
- **No analytics, ads or crash reporting.** No third-party SDKs that collect data are included.
- **On-device settings only.** Lull saves which features you want sleep mode to switch and when sleep mode started. This stays in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall.
- **Shizuku.** Lull asks Shizuku, an app you install separately, to switch Wi-Fi, mobile data, Bluetooth, battery saver and Do Not Disturb. Lull runs only those fixed commands.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/btbaazlaunda/alockscreen/issues.
